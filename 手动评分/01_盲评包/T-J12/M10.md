## T-J12｜同题六语矩阵 · English

### Round 1: English (about 600 words)

The removal men had already carried the sofa down the narrow stairs. The flat was stripped bare, echoing with every step on the unvarnished floorboards. David walked back into the empty bedroom to do a final sweep, checking the tops of the built-in wardrobes.

In the far corner, pushed against the dusty plasterwork, sat a shoebox wrapped in cracking brown parcel tape.

He brought it down to the windowsill where the afternoon light was still good. The tape tore away with a dry, papery rip.

Inside lay an old mechanical stopwatch, its chrome casing tarnished to a dull pewter. Beside it was a silver whistle on a braided nylon cord, and an official race bib from the 2004 Great North Run, crinkled and yellowed around the safety-pin holes, bearing the number 4108.

David picked up the stopwatch. The glass face was scratched, but when he pressed the knurled crown with his thumb, the internal spring gave a sharp, metallic click. The thin second hand jerked forward, sweeping across the dial with a steady, rapid tick.

Twenty years ago, his father had stood at the finish line in South Shields, clicking this exact crown as David crossed the wet tarmac in the pouring rain. His father had died the following spring, and in the chaos of clearing the house and selling the family business, the race, the time, and the stopwatch had simply evaporated from memory.

David stood by the window, watching the second hand make one complete revolution.

"David! That's the last of the boxes!" his wife called from the stairwell below.

David pressed the crown again. The hand snapped back to zero with a quiet snap. He slipped the stopwatch into his coat pocket and closed the bedroom door.

### Round 2: 中文文学翻译

搬家工人已经把沙发抬下了狭窄的楼梯。公寓被彻底搬空了，在没有上漆的地板上每走一步都会激起空荡的回声。戴维走回空无一人的卧室进行最后一遍清扫，顺手检查了嵌入式衣柜的顶端。

在最里面的角落里，紧贴着落满灰尘的石膏墙面，放着一只用发脆的棕色胶带包裹着的鞋盒。

他把盒子拿到了窗台上，那里的午后光线依然明亮。胶带被撕开时，发出干燥清脆的撕裂声。

盒子里放着一块老式机械秒表，镀铬外壳已经氧化成了暗淡的铅灰色。旁边是一只挂在编织尼龙绳上的银色口哨，以及一张二零零四年“大北跑”半程马拉松的官方号码布，别针孔周围泛黄褶皱，上面印着数字 4108。

戴维拿起秒表。表蒙玻璃上有划痕，但当他用大拇指按下带滚花的表冠时，内部的发条发出了一声清脆的金属弹响。纤细的秒针猛地向前跳动，以稳定而急促的节律划过表盘。

二十年前，父亲曾站在南希尔兹终点线的暴雨中，在戴维冲过湿滑柏油路面的那一刻按下了这个表冠。父亲在次年春天病逝，而在随后清理老宅与变卖家族小生意的混乱中，那场比赛、那个成绩以及这块秒表，便彻底从记忆中蒸发了。

戴维站在窗前，看着秒针完整地转动了一整圈。

“戴维！这是最后一批箱子了！”妻子的声音从楼下的楼梯间传来。

戴维再次按下表冠。秒针啪的一声归零。他把秒表滑进大衣口袋，轻轻关上了卧室的门。
