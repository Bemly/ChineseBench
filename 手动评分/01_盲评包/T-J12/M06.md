## T-J12｜同题六语矩阵 · English

### Moving Day

The box was open.

Tom rummaged through old clothes, books, things he couldn't remember owning.

Then his hand hit something hard.

He pulled it out. A tin box. Rusted. His childhood piggy bank.

He opened the lid. Coins. A folded piece of paper. A marble.

He unfolded the paper. His own handwriting, crooked and childish:

"I want to be an astronaut."

Tom looked at the paper. He was thirty-two now. He drove a truck.

He folded the paper back. Put it in his pocket. The marble too. The coins he poured onto the table — clinking, scattering.

He went back to the box.

### 中文翻译

箱子开了。

汤姆在旧衣服、书和一些他想不起来的东西里翻找。

然后他的手碰到了什么硬的东西。

他拿出来。一个铁盒子。生锈了。他小时候的储蓄罐。

他打开盖子。硬币。一张折起来的纸。一颗弹珠。

他展开纸。他自己的字，歪歪扭扭的，像小孩写的：

"我想当宇航员。"

汤姆看着这张纸。他现在三十二岁了。他开卡车。

他把纸折回去。放进口袋。弹珠也是。硬币他倒在桌上——叮叮当当，散了一桌。

他回到箱子。
