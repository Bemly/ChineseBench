## T-J12｜同题六语矩阵 · English

### 第一轮：英文短篇

The box was taped shut with three strips of packing tape, yellowed at the edges. David didn't remember writing "STUDY — MISC" on it in marker, but it was his handwriting. He cut the tape with a kitchen knife.

Inside: a stapler that no longer stapled. A phone charger for a phone he hadn't owned in six years. Three pens, dried out. A stack of postcards held together with a rubber band.

He picked up the postcards. The rubber band crumbled in his hand.

There were five of them. The first showed a beach in Thailand — white sand, turquoise water, a longtail boat. On the back, in his own handwriting: "Phuket, Jan 2012. The water is warm. Wish you were here."

He didn't remember writing that. He didn't remember who "you" was.

The second postcard was from Barcelona. The third from Prague. The fourth from somewhere he couldn't read — the ink had faded. The fifth was blank.

He turned the blank one over. Nothing on the back. Just a white postcard, unused, with a picture of a mountain on the front. He didn't recognize the mountain.

He sat on the floor of the empty apartment, holding the postcards. The movers had taken everything else. The walls were bare. The afternoon light came through the window at a low angle.

He tried to remember the trip to Phuket. He could picture the beach. The water. The boat. But he couldn't remember who he was with. He couldn't remember if he was alone.

He tried to remember buying the postcards. Why buy them if you're not going to send them? Why write "Wish you were here" and then not mail it?

He put the postcards back in the box. He taped it shut. He wrote "KEEP" on the side in large letters.

Then he changed his mind. He crossed out "KEEP" and wrote "DONATE."

Then he changed his mind again. He crossed that out too and wrote "KEEP."

He carried the box to the car. He put it in the trunk, behind the other boxes. He closed the trunk.

He drove to the new apartment. He carried the boxes up. He unpacked the kitchen first, then the bedroom. He left the box marked "KEEP" in the closet, behind his winter coats.

He didn't open it again.

### 第二轮：中文翻译

箱子用三条打包带封着，边缘已经发黄。他不记得自己用马克笔在上面写过"书房——杂物"，但那确实是他的字迹。他用美工刀划开胶带。

里面：一个已经订不动的订书机。一根他六年前那部手机的充电器。三支写不出水的水笔。一叠用橡皮筋捆着的明信片。

他拿起明信片。橡皮筋在手里碎了。

一共五张。第一张是泰国的海滩——白色沙滩，蓝绿色的水，一艘长尾船。背面，他自己的字迹："普吉岛，2012年1月。水很温暖。希望你也在。"

他不记得写过这句话。也不记得那个"你"是谁。

第二张来自巴塞罗那。第三张来自布拉格。第四张来自某个他已经看不清的地方——墨水褪色了。第五张是空白的。

他翻过那张空白的。背面什么都没有。就是一张白明信片，没用过，正面印着一座山。他不认识那座山。

他坐在空公寓的地板上，手里攥着那些明信片。搬家的工人已经把别的东西都搬走了。墙上光秃秃的。下午的光线从窗户斜斜地照进来。

他试着回忆普吉岛那趟旅行。他能看见海滩。水。船。但想不起来是和谁一起去的。想不起来是不是一个人。

他试着回忆为什么要买这些明信片。买了又不寄，图什么？写了"希望你又不寄出去，为什么？

他把明信片放回箱子。重新封上胶带。在侧面写了两个大字："留着。"

然后他改了主意。划掉"留着"，写上"捐了。"

然后又改回来。把"捐了"也划掉，重新写上"留着。"

他把箱子搬到车上。放在后备箱，压在其他箱子下面。关上后备箱。

他开车到新公寓。把箱子搬上楼。先拆厨房的，再拆卧室的。那个写着"留着"的箱子，他放在衣柜里，压在大衣后面。

他没有再打开过。
