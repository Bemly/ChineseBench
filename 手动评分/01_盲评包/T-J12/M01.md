## T-J12｜同题六语矩阵 · English

### 第一轮：英文原创

**The Box**

The box was at the bottom of the closet, behind a pile of old coats. It was small, cardboard, with the corners crushed. I pulled it out and sat on the floor.

Inside was a photograph, a ticket stub, and a letter.

The photograph showed a woman I didn't recognize. She was standing in front of a tree, smiling. On the back, someone had written: "Summer, 1994."

The ticket stub was for a movie. "Jurassic Park." June 15, 1993. I remembered the movie. I didn't remember going to see it.

The letter was addressed to me. The handwriting was familiar, but I couldn't place it.

"Dear," it said. "By the time you read this, I'll be gone. Don't look for me. Don't be sad. I just wanted you to know that the time we spent together was the best time of my life."

I read the letter twice. Then I folded it and put it back in the box.

Outside, the movers were carrying furniture to the truck. The sun was setting. The room was half empty.

I put the box in a bag and carried it to my car.

I didn't throw it away.

### 第二轮：中译

**盒子**

箱子在衣柜底部，一堆旧外套后面。很小，纸板做的，角都压扁了。我把它拉出来，坐在地板上。

里面是一张照片、一张票根和一封信。

照片上是一个我不认识的女人。她站在一棵树前，笑着。背面有人写着："夏天，1994年。"

票根是电影票的。《侏罗纪公园》。1993年6月15日。我记得这部电影。我不记得去看过。

信是写给我的。字迹很熟悉，但我想不起来是谁。

"亲爱的，"信上写着，"当你读到这封信的时候，我已经走了。不要找我。不要难过。我只是想告诉你，我们在一起的那段时间，是我这辈子最快乐的时光。"

我把信读了两遍。然后折好放回盒子里。

外面，搬家工人正在往卡车上搬家具。太阳正在落山。房间空了一半。

我把盒子装进一个袋子里，拎到车上。

我没有扔掉它。
