## 第 1 轮　Write a short story of about 600 words

### One Shoe

　　We found it on the second day, in a box marked MISC, which was the box everything went into that had no other box.

　　It was a small shoe.

　　Not a baby shoe. A running shoe, size twenty-four, white mesh, with the lace missing. It had been at the top of the box, under a lamp with no bulb and a wooden spoon.

　　——

　　I knew whose it was before I knew what year it was from.

　　——

　　Nora ran until she was nine. She ran until she was eleven. Then she stopped, the way children do, and by the time she stopped the shoes had stayed the same for two years and neither of us had replaced them.

　　She grew out of them in November. She was ten, or eleven. She put on the new ones and took the old ones off at the door, in the hall, in front of the tree, and I picked them up and put them in the box in the closet, the box with the lid.

　　That was thirteen years ago.

　　——

　　I looked for the other one.

　　Not that morning. I want to be clear about that. That morning I put the shoe in the box and went back to the tape.

　　It was in November of the next year that I looked. In the new house, in the hall closet, in a box I had not opened, at one in the morning, for no reason.

　　It wasn't there.

　　——

　　I checked the closet in the old house, which was not mine anymore. I checked the garage. I checked the drawer with the batteries and the keys and the things from the drawer of a life.

　　——

　　The box was in her room. It was the box of things from the year of the divorce, which is not a real thing, but the label says it.

　　There were school photographs in it. There were two shirts. There was a pair of jeans she never wore again.

　　The other shoe was not there.

　　——

　　——

　　Here is the part I have thought about.

　　She was nine. She ran. She had two shoes. Then one day she had new shoes, and one of the old ones ended up in a box in a hall closet, and then that closet was emptied, and then we moved, and then I found it, and then I looked for the other one.

　　That is what happened. That is all of it.

　　——

　　But I have wondered, standing in the garage, holding a size twenty-four shoe, whether she took it.

　　Whether she kept it. Not on purpose. Things get kept the way keys get kept, in a drawer, for no reason, for years, and then the drawer is a box in a closet, and the box is a house, and the house is sold.

　　——

　　Whether she threw it away.

　　That is the other one. That is also a way it can go. A kid does not always save the thing. A kid throws the shoe in a bin on a street, or gives it to a neighbour's dog, or puts it down on a pavement and walks on with the new ones.

　　——

　　I do not know.

　　——

　　I am fifty-one. I have moved nine times. I have, at this point, a fairly clear idea of what a person can carry and what a person cannot, and the shoe is not heavy, and it is not worth anything, and it does not have a job.

　　And I have put it in the new box.

　　Not the attic. The new box.

　　——

　　Nora is at university in another city now. She does not run.

　　——

　　The shoe is on top, under the lamp with no bulb.

　　——

　　——

　　It is the only thing in the box I know where is.

## 第 2 轮　请把你刚写的这篇小说翻译成中文

### 一只鞋

　　我们在第二天找到了它，装在一个写着"杂物"的箱子里——凡是找不到别的箱子装的，都往这个箱子里放。

　　那是一只小鞋。

　　不是婴儿鞋。是一双跑鞋，二十四周码，白色网面，鞋带没了。

　　它在箱子上层，压在一只没装灯泡的台灯和一柄木勺下面。

　　——

　　在认出它是谁的之前，我已经知道它是哪一年的。

　　——

　　诺拉一直跑到九岁。一直跑到十一岁。然后她不跑了，孩子都是这样。等她停下来的时候，那双鞋已经两年没换过了，我们两个谁也没换。

　　她是在十一月穿不下的。那时候她十岁，或者十一岁。她在门口换上新鞋，脱下旧鞋——在门厅里，在那棵树前面——我把它们捡起来，放进壁橱里那个带盖子的箱子。

　　那是十三年前。

　　——

　　我去找了另外一只。

　　不是那天早上。我要把这件事说清楚。那天早上我把鞋放进箱子里，回去接着缠胶带。

　　是第二年十一月我去找的。在新房子里，在门厅的壁橱里，在一个我没打开过的箱子里，凌晨一点钟，没有理由。

　　它不在那儿。

　　——

　　我翻了老房子的壁橱，那已经不是我的了。我翻了车库。我翻了那个放电池、钥匙和从抽屉底下来的东西的抽屉。

　　——

　　那只箱子在她房间里。那是离婚那年东西的箱子——这世上并没有"离婚那年"这么一个东西，可是标签上是这么写的。

　　里面有些小学的照片。有两件衬衫。有一条她再没穿过的牛仔裤。

　　另外一只鞋不在里面。

　　——

　　——

　　我一直在想的是这一段。

　　她九岁。她在跑步。她有两只鞋。后来有一天她有了新鞋，旧鞋里有一只进了门厅壁橱的箱子，然后那个壁橱被清空，然后我们搬家，然后我找到了它，然后我去找了另外一只。

　　事情就是这么发生的。全部情况就是这样。

　　——

　　可是我站在车库里，手里拿着一只二十四周码的鞋，想起过一个问题。

　　是不是她拿走了。

　　是不是她留着。不是故意留的。东西被留下来就像钥匙被留下来一样，放在抽屉里，没有理由，放很多年，然后那个抽屉变成了壁橱里的一个箱子，然后那个箱子变成了一栋房子，然后那栋房子卖了。

　　——

　　还是她扔了。

　　这是另外一种可能。孩子并不总会留着那样东西。孩子会把它扔进街边的垃圾箱，或者送给邻居家的狗，或者放在地上，穿着新鞋走了。

　　——

　　我不知道。

　　——

　　我五十一了。我搬过九次家。到这个年纪，对一个人能带走什么、留不住什么，我大概已经有数了。这只鞋不重，不值什么钱，也没有用处。

　　可我还是把它放进了新箱子。

　　不是阁楼。是新箱子。

　　——

　　诺拉现在在另一个城市的大学里。她不跑步了。

　　——

　　那只鞋在最上层，压在那盏没装灯泡的灯下面。

　　——

　　——

　　这是整箱东西里，我唯一知道它在哪儿的一件。
